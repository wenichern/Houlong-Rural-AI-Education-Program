# Part A: English

## 1. Executive Summary

We propose a multi-year program that brings AI and machine learning into the classrooms of rural schools around the Houlong Industrial Park (334 hectares, covering parts of Haibao, Wanbao and Dongming villages, Miaoli County). The program starts with teachers, builds student AI literacy through the six AIPACK subject areas, and ties lessons to local livelihoods so that the next generation can use modern technology to extend, not replace, their community's legacy.

The design borrows its structure from Massachusetts (teacher-first professional development, then a student curriculum pilot, backed by clear classroom guidance) and plugs into Taiwan's existing AIPACK framework so it aligns with what the Ministry of Education already supports.

## 2. Background and Rationale

**Massachusetts model.** The state launched *Future Ready: AI in the Classroom*, a professional development pilot with Project Lead The Way covering AI fundamentals, ethical use and ongoing collaboration with industry experts. A follow-up semester-long student course requires no prior computer science experience. The state education department also issued guidance on transparency, approved-tool lists and parent notification.

**Taiwan AIPACK.** AIPACK (Artificial Intelligence Pedagogical Content Knowledge) is a framework for how teachers apply AI in their subject teaching. Sample lessons are developed for six areas: Chinese, English, mathematics, science, art and special education. The original program used research base schools, each producing at least two AIPACK lesson plans with teaching-practice records. In 2026 the Ministry established the Smart Education Teacher Education Alliance to build on this work.

**Local need.** The Houlong Industrial Park is a government focus area. Nearby small schools have few teachers and limited resources. Education is the most durable entry point for rural AI adoption, and local livelihoods give lessons real meaning.

## 4. Objectives

1. Train a cohort of local teachers in AIPACK-style AI-integrated teaching.
2. Produce a bank of locally grounded AI-integrated lesson plans across the six AIPACK subjects.
3. Build student AI literacy, including ethics and safe use, from elementary to senior high.
4. Connect students to local livelihoods through community-based projects.
5. Leave behind materials and a teacher community that can continue without outside support.

## 5. Program Design

- **Layer 1, Teachers (core):** workshops, shared lesson design and a teacher community across all five lower schools, since each school alone is too small to sustain one.
- **Layer 2, Students:** subject lessons with AI integration, plus a short AI literacy unit adapted from the Massachusetts approach.
- **Layer 3, Community:** interviews with farmers, fishers, makers and business owners; student projects that apply AI to real local needs.
- **Governance:** an approved-tool list, parent notification, data privacy rules and human oversight, following the Massachusetts guidance model and Taiwan's own education-AI guidelines.

## 8. Curriculum Mapping Method

Massachusetts AI-literacy and computer-science learning goals are used as a reference, not copied. Each goal is mapped to Taiwan's 12-year national curriculum (108課綱) domains, then written as an AIPACK-format lesson plan with both teaching and assessment design. Local language and cultural context (for example Hakka and Hoklo heritage) is reviewed with teachers and community members.

# Part B: 繁體中文

## 一、計畫摘要

本計畫擬於後龍產業園區（占地334公頃，涵蓋海寶里、灣寶里及東明里部分地區，苗栗縣）周邊偏鄉學校，推動為期多年的人工智慧（AI）與機器學習教育。計畫從教師增能出發，透過AIPACK六大學科培養學生AI素養，並結合在地產業與生活，讓下一代善用新科技延續並拓展社區的傳統與特色。

計畫架構參考美國麻薩諸塞州「教師優先、再推學生課程試辦、並有明確課堂指引」的作法，同時銜接我國教育部既有的AIPACK架構，使計畫與政府現行推動方向一致。

## 二、背景與理由

\*\*麻薩諸塞州模式：\*\*該州與Project Lead The Way合作推出「Future Ready: AI in the Classroom」教師專業發展試辦計畫，內容包含AI基礎、倫理使用，以及與產業專家和同儕的持續交流。後續推出為期一學期、不需電腦科學先備知識的學生課程。州教育廳亦發布指引，強調透明、核准工具清單及通知家長。

\*\*臺灣AIPACK：\*\*AIPACK（人工智慧學科教學知識）是協助教師在學科教學中應用AI的專業知識框架，已研發國語、英語、數學、自然、藝術及特教六個領域的範例課程。原計畫以研究基地學校為單位，每校至少完成兩份教案並提交教學實踐資料。教育部並於2026年成立「智慧教育師資培育聯盟」，延續相關成果。

\*\*在地需求：\*\*後龍產業園區為政府重點發展區域，周邊小型學校教師人力與資源有限。教育是偏鄉導入AI最穩固的起點，而在地生計能讓課程更具意義。

## 四、計畫目標

1. 培訓在地教師，具備AIPACK式AI融入教學能力。
2. 產出貼近在地、涵蓋六大學科的AI融入教案庫。
3. 從國小到高中建立學生AI素養，包含倫理與安全使用。
4. 透過社區專題，讓學生連結在地生計。
5. 留下教材與教師社群，使計畫在外部資源退出後仍能持續。

## 五、計畫設計

- \*\*第一層，教師（核心）：\*\*辦理工作坊、共同備課，並建立涵蓋五所中小學的跨校教師社群，因為單一學校規模太小，難以自行維持。
- \*\*第二層，學生：\*\*學科融入AI的課程，並參考麻州作法設計簡短的AI素養單元。
- \*\*第三層，社區：\*\*訪談農民、漁民、工藝師與店家，讓學生以AI解決真實的在地問題。
- \*\*治理機制：\*\*核准工具清單、家長通知、資料隱私規範及人為監督，參考麻州指引與我國教育AI相關規範。

## 八、課程對應方法
